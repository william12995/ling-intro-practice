# Morphology Practice: How to Use It

You can access the practice here from any browser, on your computer or phone:

https://william12995.github.io/ling-intro-practice/ch01_morphology/

There are 39 questions in 7 sections. The questions start with basic exercises and gradually become more challenging, with the last sections using data from Hebrew, Turkish, and Haitian Creole.

## Getting started

Enter your student ID (one letter followed by 8 digits, e.g. B13012345) and your name, then click **Start**. Please use your real student ID, since your answers will be recorded under it.

## Answering an item

For each question, first choose or enter your answer. There are four types of questions:

* **Multiple choice:** choose one option.
* **Select all that apply:** select all the correct options. You can also leave all options unselected if you think none are correct.
* **Segmentation:** click between two letters to divide the word. Click the same spot again to remove the boundary.
* **Fill in:** type your answer in the box. You can use the buttons below the box to enter dʒ, ı, and ʃ. You can also type c for dʒ and i for ı.

After choosing your answer, **write a short explanation of your reasoning (20–600 characters)**. Tell us how you arrived at your answer—for example, which words you compared or what evidence you used. One or two clear sentences are enough.

Then click **Submit**. After submitting, your answer and reasoning cannot be changed. You will then see the correct answer and an explanation.

You need to write your reasoning **before** seeing the explanation. This is intentional: the goal is to show your own analysis rather than simply following the provided explanation.

## AI feedback

After you submit an answer, an AI model (Google Gemini) will give you a short comment on your reasoning. It checks whether your reasoning actually supports your answer. In other words, you can get the correct answer for the wrong reason, and the feedback may point that out.

A few things to keep in mind:

* Only your answer and reasoning are sent to the AI. Your name and student ID are not.
* The AI feedback is for reference only and may sometimes be incorrect. The answer key was also prepared with the help of AI.
* If something seems wrong, please ask your TA.
* If you disagree with the feedback, you can click **I disagree with this feedback** and explain why. Your TA will be able to see your response.
* The AI feedback does not affect your score.
* If you see **AI feedback isn't available right now**, the service may be temporarily busy. Your answer has still been recorded, and you can click **Try again** later.

## Progress and retries

Once your answers have been saved, you will see **Answers recorded** at the top of the page. If it says **Uploading**, please wait a moment and don't close the page.

If you see a message saying that some answers were not accepted, please let your TA know.

After finishing a section, you can click **Try again** to practice the questions again. Your first attempt for each question will remain on record.

Your progress on the page is saved in your browser. If you switch to another device or clear your browser data, the page may start from the beginning, but your submitted answers will still be recorded.

If you are using a shared computer, please click **Switch student** when you are finished so that the next student can sign in.

## Questions

If you have any questions, feel free to ask your TA in class or on NTU COOL.
