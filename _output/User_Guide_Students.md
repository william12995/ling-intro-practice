# Morphology Practice: How to Use It

Open this link in any browser, on a phone or a computer:

https://william12995.github.io/ling-intro-practice/ch01_morphology/

There are 39 items in 7 sections. The sections start easy and get harder, ending with data from Hebrew, Turkish and Haitian Creole.

## Getting started

Enter your student ID (one letter followed by 8 digits, e.g. B13012345) and your name, then click **Start**. Use your real student ID. Your answers are recorded under it.

## Answering an item

1. **Choose your answer.** There are four kinds of items:
    - Multiple choice: pick one option.
    - Select all that apply: pick every correct option. Picking none is allowed.
    - Segmentation: click the gap between two letters to cut the word there. Click the gap again to undo.
    - Fill in: type your answer. Use the buttons under the box to insert dʒ, ı and ʃ, or type c or j for dʒ and i for ı.
2. **Write your reasoning** (20 to 600 characters). Explain how you got the answer: which words you compared, or what evidence you used. One or two clear sentences are enough.
3. **Click Submit.** Once you submit, your answer and reasoning are locked. You then see whether you were right and an explanation.

You have to write your reasoning before you see the explanation. That order is on purpose. The point is to show your own thinking, not to copy the explanation.

## AI feedback

After you submit, an AI model (Google Gemini) comments on your reasoning in a few sentences. It checks whether your reasoning actually supports your answer. A correct answer can rest on a wrong argument, and the feedback will tell you if that happened.

- Only your answer and reasoning are sent to the AI. Your name and student ID are not.
- **The feedback is written by AI and is for reference only.** It can be wrong. The answer key on this page was also drafted with AI help. If something looks wrong, ask your TA.
- If you think the feedback is wrong, click **I disagree with this feedback** and say why. Your TA will read it.
- The AI feedback does not change your score for the item.
- If you see "AI feedback isn't available right now", the service is busy. Your answer has still been recorded. You can click **Try again** later.

## Progress and retries

- At the top of the page you'll see **Answers recorded** once your answers have been saved. If it says **Uploading**, wait a moment and don't close the page.
- If you see a message that some answers were not accepted, tell your TA.
- After finishing a section you can click **Try again** to redo it for practice. Your first attempt at each item stays on record.
- Your progress on the page is saved in your browser. If you switch devices or clear your browser data, the page starts over, but everything you already submitted is still on record.
- If you're on a shared computer, click **Switch student** when you're done so the next person can sign in.
